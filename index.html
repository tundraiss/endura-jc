Production stages — automatic scheduling

Pretreatment is planned 3 working days before completion, powder coating 2, and curing/QC 1. Dates exclude weekends but not public holidays. Stage tasks disappear after their status is advanced.

Oven capacity — curing schedule

Capacity is measured in estimated oven loads, not job count. Change the shared oven settings and each job's expected number of oven loads. Days exceeding capacity are highlighted. Set operating hours and minutes per batch, then use Find slot to suggest a weekday with room. Confirm before any date is changed.
